Our Process

A structured, transparent approach to every engagement.

Scoping
Define targets, rules of engagement, and timeline. NDA signed before any work begins.
Reconnaissance
Passive and active information gathering. OSINT, subdomain enumeration, surface mapping.
Assessment
Systematic testing of identified attack vectors. Manual and automated techniques combined.
Exploitation
Controlled proof-of-concept exploitation to confirm impact. No damage, no data exfiltration.
Reporting
Full written report with severity ratings, evidence, and remediation steps.
Remediation
Post-fix verification included. We confirm the vulnerabilities are properly patched.
